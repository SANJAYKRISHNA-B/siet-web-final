AUTHENTIC SPACE HERITAGE

Sri Shakthi Space Mission

SRI SHAKTHI IS THE ONLY PRIVATE ENGINEERING COLLEGE IN INDIA, WHO HAD LAUNCHED THEIR OWN SATELLITE THROUGH ISRO. Our student-built nano-satellite, SriShakthiSat (460g), was placed into a 505 km Sun-Synchronous Orbit aboard ISRO's PSLV-C51 mission on February 28, 2021. Sri Shakthi also operates a state-of-the-art satellite ground station on campus, formally inaugurated by former ISRO Chairman Dr. K. Sivan.
