## 1. Overview
"My Dream India" is an annual state-level youth leadership and essay competition organized by Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology. The competition encourages students of **10th, 11th, and 12th Standard** across schools in Tamil Nadu and India to share their bold vision, research, and roadmap for a self-reliant, developed nation.

## 2. Key Highlights
- **Eligibility:** 10th, 11th & 12th Standard (All boards: State Board, CBSE, ICSE, Matriculation)
- **Languages:** English and Tamil (ஆங்கிலம் மற்றும் தமிழ்)
- **Word Limit:** 1,000 – 1,500 words (typed or neatly handwritten & scanned)
- **Format:** PDF, DOCX, or clear image scan

## 3. Grand Reward: The Wellington & Nilgiri Expedition
- **Top 100 Ranking Students** receive an exclusive leadership and educational exposure journey.
- Traveled aboard the UNESCO World Heritage **Nilgiri Mountain Railway (Toy Train)** to **The Madras Regimental Centre, Wellington (Coonoor / Ooty)**.
- **100% Fully Sponsored by Sri Shakthi:** Travel, toy train tickets, accommodation, and hospitality expenses are completely borne by the institution.
- **Mentors Honored:** Faculty mentors and guiding teachers from participating schools receive Certificates of Appreciation.
- **Recognition for All:** Every registered student receives an official Certificate of Participation.

## 4. Essay Themes
1. **India 2047: Technology, Innovation & Youth Leadership**
2. **Empowering Rural India through Smart Engineering & AI**
3. **Atmanirbhar Defense, Space & Aerospace Leadership (Atmanirbhar Bharat)**
4. **Universal Quality Education & Equal Opportunities for Every Child**
5. **Clean, Green & Climate-Resilient Nation**
