Journey to The Madras Regimental Centre, Wellington via Nilgiri Toy Train

The top 100 ranking students will travel aboard the legendary UNESCO World Heritage Nilgiri Mountain Railway (Toy Train) to Wellington (Coonoor / Ooty) to visit the historic Madras Regimental Centre — the oldest infantry regiment of the Indian Army.

  • ✓ 100% Borne by Sri Shakthi: Total travel, Nilgiri Mountain Railway tickets, accommodation, and food fully sponsored by SIET.
  • ✓ Exclusive Military Heritage Tour: Visit the War Memorial, Regimental Museum, and experience firsthand the discipline and courage of our armed forces.
  • ✓ Leadership Mentorship Sessions: Direct interaction with distinguished defense officers, academicians, and nation-builders.
  • ✓ Honoring Faculty Mentors: All guiding teachers from participating schools receive special Certificates of Appreciation.

CONTEST THEMES & TOPICS

Themes for My Dream India

Choose any one of the following themes to express your vision, research, and actionable roadmap for a prosperous, self-reliant India.

THEME 01

India 2047: Technology, Innovation & Youth Leadership

How artificial intelligence, clean technology, and youthful enterprise can propel India to become a global superpower by its centenary year of independence.

💡 Focus: Tech Sovereignty & Innovation
THEME 02

Empowering Rural India through Smart Engineering

Innovative solutions in precision agriculture, water preservation, affordable rural healthcare, and local renewable energy microgrids.

🌾 Focus: Sustainable Rural Progress
THEME 03

Atmanirbhar Defense, Space & Aerospace Leadership

Reflecting on India's defense valor and indigenous milestones (such as Sri Shakthi's own SriShakthiSat launch via ISRO) to safeguard national sovereignty.

🛰️ Focus: Defense & Space Self-Reliance
THEME 04

Universal Quality Education & Equal Opportunities

Bridging urban-rural digital divides, nurturing STEM research among youth, and cultivating critical thinking in schools across all communities.

📚 Focus: Value-Based Modern Education
THEME 05

Clean, Green & Climate-Resilient Nation

Strategies to eliminate single-use plastics, accelerate electric mobility, protect Indian biodiversity, and lead global climate-positive stewardship.

🌱 Focus: Environment & Sustainability
THEME 06

Word Limit

1,000 to 1,500 words. Essays can be typed or neatly handwritten & scanned.

Languages Accepted

English or Tamil. Both mediums receive equal evaluation weightage.

Accepted File Formats

PDF, DOC, DOCX, or high-clarity image scan (PNG/JPG up to 10MB).

Originality Mandate

Submissions must be original thoughts of the student. AI or copied text is disqualified.
