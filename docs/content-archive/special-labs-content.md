### Laboratory 07: PCB Design & Assembly Research Lab (`pcb-design-assembly-lab`)
- **Source Slides:** Slide 13 (`Advance Research Labs Final Template.pptx`), Slide 9 (`Advance Research Labs.pptx`)
- **Department:** Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering / EEE
- **Tagline:** Electronics Prototyping — From design to real-world prototypes.
- **Three-Stage Rapid Prototyping Pipeline:**
  - **Stage 01 — Circuit Design:** Professional PCB layout and schematic capture using industry-grade EDA tools (Altium Designer, KiCad, EAGLE).
  - **Stage 02 — Assembly:** In-house PCB assembly line for Surface Mount Technology (SMT) manufacturing, stencil solder paste application, and component placement.
  - **Stage 03 — Testing & Debugging:** Complete electrical validation, thermal analysis, and signal troubleshooting of electronic prototypes.
- **Lab Purpose:** Accelerate the transition from circuit concept to validated physical hardware.
- **Visual Assets:** `docs/content-archive/source-assets/pcb-design-assembly-lab/pptx-pcb-design.webp`, `pptx-pcb-assembly.webp`
