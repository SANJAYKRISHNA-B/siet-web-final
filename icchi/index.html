About the conference

Where computational intelligence meets healthcare

The 7th International Conference on Computational and Healthcare Innovations (ICCHI 2027) will be held at Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, organised by the Department of Biomedical Engineering in association with Biomedical Engineering Society of India (BMESI).

This International Conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, scientists, healthcare professionals, industry experts, research scholars and students from around the world to share their latest research findings, innovations, technologies and methodologies in the rapidly evolving fields of Computational Intelligence and Healthcare.

ICCHI 2027 provides a platform to explore the convergence of Artificial Intelligence, Computational Intelligence, Biomedical Engineering, Medical Imaging, Biomedical Signal Processing, Healthcare Informatics, Medical Devices, Medical Instrumentation, Internet of Medical Things, Computational Biology, Biomaterials and Precision Medicine.

This conference aims to

  • Share the latest research findings, technologies and methodologies.
  • Facilitate cross-disciplinary collaboration among clinicians, researchers and engineers.
  • Introduce novel diagnostic tools and techniques.
  • Showcase cutting-edge biomedical devices and applications.
  • Build international research networks and partnerships.
  • Create professional development opportunities for students and researchers.

About the institution & department

Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology

2006EstablishedNAAC “A”Accredited9NBA-accredited depts.14 / 7 / 6UG / PG / Ph.D.

Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology (SIET) was established in the year 2006. SIET, an Autonomous institution, is approved by All India Council for Technical Education, New Delhi, and is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. The institute is accredited by NAAC with “A” grade. Nine departments – BME, BT, ECE, EEE, CSE, IT, FT, Mechanical Engineering and Agricultural Engineering – are NBA accredited.

SIET was founded by Dr. S. Thangavelu, an academician with 28 years of experience in TNAU, with the motto of “Powering the Youth; Empowering the Nation”. The institute currently offers 14 UG programs, 7 PG programs and 6 Ph.D. programs. It has secured research projects totalling about Rs. 200 lakhs from major government funding agencies including FIST-SERB, DST-NRDMS and DAE-BRNS.

Department of Biomedical Engineering

The Department of Biomedical Engineering was established in 2016 with the vision of integrating Medical Sciences and Advanced Engineering to address challenges in clinical healthcare. The department emphasizes industry-oriented education and interdisciplinary research through collaborations with leading hospitals, research centers, and diagnostic centers. Equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and experienced faculty members, it provides students with strong technical knowledge and practical exposure.
