## 🛰️ Institutional Space Credential

> **Sri Shakthi is the only private engineering college in India who had launched their own satellite through ISRO.**

- **Satellite:** SriShakthiSat (460g Pico-Satellite)
- **Launch Vehicle:** ISRO PSLV-C51 (Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR)
- **Launch Date:** 28th February 2021
- **Orbit:** 505 km Sun-Synchronous Polar Orbit (SSO)
- **Ground Station:** Dedicated Sri Shakthi Satellite Ground Station on SIET campus, inaugurated by then-ISRO Chairman Dr. K. Sivan.
